🔍 Why do different job titles under the same SSOC code have different market compensation?

Official statistical classifications like SSOC 2024 group many related job titles under a single 5-digit occupational code (e.g., SSOC 11110 encompasses Cabinet Minister, Attorney General, and Legislator / Member of Parliament; SSOC 12111 encompasses Chief Financial Officer, Finance Director, and Assistant Finance Manager). MOM publishes survey data only at the 5-digit code level and never at the individual sub-title level.

In real Singapore corporate and public sector practice, an apex Cabinet Minister or Chief Financial Officer earns substantially more than a backbench MP or Assistant Manager. The Singapore Career Actuarial Engine resolves this disparity by applying Statutory Gazetted Benchmarks to public roles and Actuarial Seniority Multipliers to private roles, ensuring every single one of the 9,449 titles reflects its true market compensation.
